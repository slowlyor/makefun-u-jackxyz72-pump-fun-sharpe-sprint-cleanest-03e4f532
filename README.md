A selective early-momentum paper-trading bot for the pump.fun replay. Instead of chasing every launch — the two-slot fill latency means the opening pump is already gone by the time an order lands — it buys only the narrow slice of launches whose early tape shows real breadth and honest creator behaviour (at least 10 SOL of real reserves, at least 3 distinct buyers, creator hasn't sold, creator bought at most 1 SOL at creation, early selling under half of early buying), sizes up when the creator's own creation-slot buy sits in the modest 0.05–1 SOL band, and exits on a +15% take-profit, a -6% stop, a single 2 SOL sell, or the creator selling, holding otherwise with no time limit.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
